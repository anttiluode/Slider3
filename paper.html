Scope / mathematical honesty

Faithful components, not a reproduced theorem

These two components are implemented using the actual manuscript equations. What is not reproduced: the full spatial current regularization Jₕ, passive Q cutoff, integration over the entire complex fiber, the quaternionic cofactor energy estimate, the global local-smoothing bound, or a proof that any physical cavity can be queried more efficiently.

For the complete current, conservation and positive semidefiniteness are tested numerically on multiple coherent phase configurations. For the planar operator, disk boundary values, coefficient dependence on direction, and Fourier equation residuals are tested. The two examples use different finite mode scales suitable for their respective coordinate charts; they are not a single coupled reproduction of the complete proof.

The question this page can answer: Can the paper-derived quantities distinguish coherent waves that have the same conventional power spectrum? Yes. Are they uniquely sensitive to such changes, or proven better than phase space, ordinary coherent intensity or much cheaper features? No. Those comparisons need a genuinely matched sensor/compute budget and a downstream prediction task.
